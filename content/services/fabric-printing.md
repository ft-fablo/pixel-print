Textile printing requires a deep understanding of fabric weaves, ink absorption, and color fastness. Pixel Print House produces high-grade branded apparel and fabric items built to retain crisp colors through countless washes.

From precision 3D puff embroidery on caps and executive polo shirts to full-bleed dye sublimation on athletic wear and custom event tablecloths, we match the ideal technique to your garment specification.

### Textile Techniques
- **Direct-to-Film (DTF) Printing**: Ultra-vivid gradients, razor-sharp photographic details, and exceptional stretch without cracking.
- **Precision Computerized Embroidery**: Tight stitch density using lustrous German threads that don't fray or fade.
- **Heavy Cotton Canvas Totes**: Premium stitched bags perfect for sustainable brand packaging.
