Corporate gifting is an extension of institutional reputation. Pixel Print House designs, curates, and customizes bespoke VIP corporate gift sets that express gratitude with elegance and restraint.

From laser-engraved metal pens and leather-bound notebooks to wireless desk sets and custom thermal drinkware, every item is branded with high-fidelity techniques and presented in tailored rigid boxes with custom-molded high-density foam inlays.

### What We Deliver
- **Turnkey Curation**: Gift selection, custom packaging, branded inserts, and ribbon finishing delivered assembled and ready to present.
- **Precision Laser Engraving**: Crisp, permanent monogramming and corporate logo etching on metals, woods, and leathers.
- **Tailored Inserts**: Custom CNC-cut EVA foam lined with velvet or matte black board to hold each gift perfectly in place.
