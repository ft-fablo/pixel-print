A business card is your first tangible handshake. Pixel Print House creates executive business cards and corporate stationery that command attention through weight, texture, and refined craftsmanship.

We offer exceptional paper weights up to 800 GSM, crafted by duplexing premium boards or using pure cotton stocks. Paired with precision letterpress, edge foiling, or raised gloss spot UV, your brand identity makes an indelible impression.

### Finishing & Customization
- **Edge Gilding & Edge Painting**: Match your corporate Pantone shade directly onto the bevelled edges of ultra-thick cards.
- **Letterpress & Blind Deboss**: Timeless tactile depressions that can be felt immediately in hand.
- **Complete Suite Matching**: Seamlessly coordinate your business cards with letterheads, envelopes, and presentation folders.
