Box packaging at **Pixel Print House** is approached with architectural precision and restraint. We focus on structure, proportion, and tactile finish to ensure packaging feels deliberate, balanced, and unmistakably premium.

From rigid presentation boxes to folding cartons and custom die-cut formats, every detail is carefully considered—material selection, board thickness, print clarity, and surface treatment all contribute to the final result. We evaluate how the box opens, how it feels in hand, and how graphics align seamlessly across edges and folds.

### Key Capabilities & Finishes
- **Rigid Box Construction**: Hand-wrapped rigid boxes with clean 90-degree edges, internal foam inserts, and magnetic snap lids.
- **Surface Treatments**: High-precision hot foil stamping, selective gloss spot UV on ultra-matte backgrounds, and deep crisp debossing.
- **Structural Integrity**: Engineered for product protection during transit while delivering a memorable unboxing experience.
